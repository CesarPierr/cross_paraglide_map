## Q1. Which convergence lines are taught at Laragne, Tallard and Saint-Vincent-les-Forts? Where and when do they form?

### Takeaway
I documented only one convergence with a source: a **convergence on the ridge of the Montagne de Chabre (Laragne)**. There, southerly thermals and breeze meet a weak northerly synoptic wind. Club safety material ties it to **violent dust devils**, especially at the Espranons takeoff. I found **no accessible primary source** for the Gap/Tallard/Céüse/Dévoluy convergence lines, the Serre-Ponçon convergence or the Ubaye convergence. They remain open gaps.

### Cited Findings
- Les Toiles du Sud's safety document "Les surprises aérologiques" describes **violent dust devils on the Montagne de Chabre (Laragne) when thermals coming from the south meet a weak northerly meteorological wind**. Rendered in French: « des dust devils violents apparaissent sur la montagne de Chabre quand les thermiques venant du sud rencontrent un vent météo de nord faible ». — [Les Toiles du Sud, « Les surprises aérologiques » (PDF)](https://lestoilesdusud-parapente.fr/images/stories/doc_public/meteo/surprisesaerologiques2.pdf)
- The same document says that **at the Espranons takeoff (Chabre), dust devils can form when the breeze takes over from the northerly meteorological wind**. These are violent whirlwinds in **stable, dry, slightly windy** conditions (« quand la brise prend le relais du vent météo de nord… conditions stables, sèches et légèrement ventées »). — [Les Toiles du Sud PDF](https://lestoilesdusud-parapente.fr/images/stories/doc_public/meteo/surprisesaerologiques2.pdf)
- The Chabre site information board (Communauté de communes Sisteronais-Buëch, 2024) lists **« dust devils »** and wind rollers (« rouleaux ») among the site's dangers. It gives the favourable conditions as **light north wind, morning thermal breeze, calm**. — [Panneau site de vol libre Laragne-Chabre (PDF)](https://www.sisteronais-buech.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Panneau_chabre-B-1.pdf); also summarised in [cataloguevollibre 05-05 Laragne](http://cataloguevollibre.free.fr/Sites-Vol-Libre/Departements/Dept-05/05-05.html)
- The Saint-Vincent-les-Forts pages talk about the **massif du Morgon**, which gives a "protected volume" (« volume protégé ») for climbing onto the high reliefs of **Dormillouse and Blanche**. They also mention cross departures in breeze conditions. Neither page names a convergence line. — [Rock the Outdoor – site de Saint-Vincent-les-Forts](https://paragliding.rocktheoutdoor.com/spots/vol-site-parapente-saint-vincent-les-forts/); [Différen'Ciel – St Vincent les Forts](https://www.differen-ciel.fr/2000/11/22/st-vincent-les-forts/)
- A Gap-Tallard gliding outfit describes the standard local glider circuit: depart toward the **Petite Céüse** to use the lift that carries you to the **Grande Céüse**, flying **along the vertical cliffs of Céüse**. Commonly reached ceilings are **2 200–2 500 m, sometimes 3 000 m**. The tour Céüse, Pic de Bure, Dévoluy, Charance takes about 1 h 15. The page does not mention a convergence. — [Planeur Gap-Tallard.FR – promenade](https://www.planeur-gaptallard.fr/promenade/)

### Inferences
- The Chabre "convergence" is a ridge-scale shear and convergence between the up-slope/up-valley flow from the Buëch/Durance side (south) and a weak synoptic north wind on the crest. It is marked by dust devils rather than a long cloud street. It sits on the crest line joining the FFVL takeoffs (Table d'orientation → Top). Timing in the sources is "when the breeze takes over", i.e. late morning to early afternoon.
- The sourced Saint-Vincent material puts the protected, lift-rich volume on the Morgon / Dormillouse / Blanche reliefs. This fits a classic confluence of the Durance and Ubaye breezes near the dam and Saint-Vincent, but no source I could reach states it.

## Q2. How strong is the Durance breeze and when? How do Mistral and other synoptic winds change the picture?

### Takeaway
The sources agree that the Durance-axis breeze is **strong in summer afternoons**. Sisteron schools quote **30–40 km/h** in the afternoon (crab or backward landings; 12h–16h "reserved for experienced pilots"). Embrun (Mont Guillaume) and Serre-Ponçon warn of a **"forte brise l'après-midi en période estivale"** and of landings **turbulent after about 14–15h**. Saint-Vincent reports breezes "particulièrement soutenues" at takeoff mid-afternoon. On Mistral/north-west days, **Saint-Vincent-les-Forts is described as one of the few flyable sites**. Dévoluy is fully exposed. Chabre flies in light N but quickly becomes too strong with waves. Aspres becomes turbulent and dangerous in strong NW.

### Cited Findings
**Durance breeze (Sisteron → Serre-Ponçon → Embrun)**
- At Sisteron, the relief of the Durance cluse makes **"very regular" valley-breeze conditions**: air is channelled and accelerated in the cluse. In summer the valley breeze **can be very strong (30–40 km/h) in the afternoon**, and landings are made **"en crabe" or reversing**. Do not fly if the breeze is too strong. In summer, flights are done **early (9–11h) or late (after 17h, the "restitution")**. Between **12h and 16h** the air is often reserved for expert pilots. — [Summits.fr, « Parapente Sisteron : voler au Rocher de la Baume »](https://www.summits.fr/article/parapente-sisteron-guide)
- At **Serre-Ponçon**, the landing becomes turbulent as soon as the breeze is strong, **"généralement après 14-15h en été"**. The (unnamed in the summary) site is **discouraged June–September because the breezes are too strong**. — [Infos-parapente, « Faire du parapente à Serre-Ponçon »](https://infos-parapente.com/parapente-serre-poncon/)
- **Embrun / Mont Guillaume**: watch out for the **« forte brise l'après-midi en période estivale »**. The takeoff is best used **« à partir de midi »**. Conditions are ideal spring to autumn "thanks to the Durance valley, known for its thermals". Landing is at the **centre de vacances de Chadenas, on the lake shore (~800 m)**. — [Serreponcon.com – parapente](https://www.serreponcon.com/parapente-serre-poncon/); [S'Air Ponçon Parapente – Mont Guillaume](https://www.sair-poncon-parapente.com/produit/vol-en-parapente-le-must-mont-guillaume-ascendance/); [Alpes-Envol – sites de l'Embrunais](https://www.alpes-envol.fr/pages/sites-de-vol-libre/l-embrunais/)
- **Saint-Vincent-les-Forts**: in summer, the **breezes at takeoff can be "particulièrement soutenues", especially mid-afternoon**. **Very good flying in late afternoon.** Favourable sectors **W and NW**. The site "guarantees" flight time thanks to an "exceptional aerological configuration". — [Rock the Outdoor – Saint-Vincent](https://paragliding.rocktheoutdoor.com/spots/vol-site-parapente-saint-vincent-les-forts/); [Différen'Ciel – St Vincent](https://www.differen-ciel.fr/2000/11/22/st-vincent-les-forts/)
- **FFVL Saint-Vincent-les-Forts takeoff [1361]**: 44.4433 N, 6.3715 E, 1 275 m. — [FFVL terrain 1361](https://federation.ffvl.fr/sites_pratique/voir/1361)

**Buëch side (Aspres)**
- **Aspres – La Longeagne Sud [1391]**: 44.5409 N, 5.7131 E, 1 526 m, commune La Faurie. Favourable sectors **SE, S, SW, W**; unfavourable **N, NE, NW**. Ideal conditions come with a **breeze regime / southerly winds**. The takeoff is "souvent bien alimenté" and **can become impossible to launch between 14h and 16h**, so strong-wind launch skills are needed. Landing is at the **aérodrome du Chevalet**: no overflying the runways, approach between the north and east runways. There are beacons on the aerodrome and on the Longeagne takeoff. — [FFVL terrain 1391](https://federation.ffvl.fr/sites_pratique/voir/1391); [Alpes-Envol – aérodrome d'Aspres-sur-Buëch](https://www.alpes-envol.fr/pages/aerodromes/aerodrome-d-aspres-sur-buech/)

**Synoptic modifiers**
- **Mistral / N (Aspres)**: « Attention à l'aérologie quand le mistral est annoncé dans la vallée du Rhône ». The site can be **turbulent and dangerous in strong NW**. — [FFVL terrain 1391](https://federation.ffvl.fr/sites_pratique/voir/1391); [Alpes-Envol – Aspres](https://www.alpes-envol.fr/pages/aerodromes/aerodrome-d-aspres-sur-buech/)
- **N (Chabre)**: favourable with **light north wind**. The **north wind very quickly becomes too strong, with frequent south or north wave formations** (« formations d'ondes »). — [Panneau Chabre](https://www.sisteronais-buech.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Panneau_chabre-B-1.pdf); [cataloguevollibre 05-05](http://cataloguevollibre.free.fr/Sites-Vol-Libre/Departements/Dept-05/05-05.html)
- **W–NW (Chabre)**: **north takeoff 1 is not recommended with a W to NW tendency**. NW wind "tends to deflect upward", and westerly effects are to be expected in front of the takeoff. — [Panneau Chabre](https://www.sisteronais-buech.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Panneau_chabre-B-1.pdf); [cataloguevollibre 05-05](http://cataloguevollibre.free.fr/Sites-Vol-Libre/Departements/Dept-05/05-05.html)
- **NW (Saint-Vincent)**: « par vent de nord-ouest c'est un des seuls sites volables du coin ». — [Rock the Outdoor – Saint-Vincent](https://paragliding.rocktheoutdoor.com/spots/vol-site-parapente-saint-vincent-les-forts/)
- **Mistral (Dévoluy)**: « Le Dévoluy n'est pas du tout abrité du mistral et ne vole que quand la météo est faible ». There can be **very strong valley breezes, especially in summer**. Valley breezes have a **southerly tendency** (creating lee zones) and run "du sud vers le nord". Calm air is mandatory, and takeoffs may need several attempts. A northerly tendency is linked to "long-lasting" cloud or fog (meaning unclear in the summary). — [Parapentiste.info forum – Superdévoluy](https://www.parapentiste.info/forum/sites-de-vols/superdevoluy-t22564.0.html); [Parapentiste.info – Dévoluy / Joue du Loup](https://www.parapentiste.info/forum/sites-de-vols/devoluy-joue-du-loup-t45026.0.html)
- **Mistral (Baronnies – Col d'Ey, Buis)**: favourable **N, NE, NW**; unfavourable E, SE, S, SW, W. « Par mistral le site est abrité mais le vent peut se renforcer brutalement, surtout au-dessus des crêtes ». — [FFVL terrain 671 Col d'Ey](https://federation.ffvl.fr/sites_pratique/voir/671)
- **N (Nyons – Garde-Grosse)**: 918 m, **favourable with W**. Takeoff is possible in N **but turbulent**. — [FFVL terrain 139 Nyons Garde-Grosse](https://federation.ffvl.fr/sites_pratique/voir/139)
- **SW (Diois – Glandasse)**: south-facing takeoff at **1 780 m** (2.5 h walk from Châtillon-en-Diois on the GR 91). Favourable with **slope breezes and south-westerly synoptic winds**. — [cataloguevollibre 26-05](http://cataloguevollibre.free.fr/Sites-Vol-Libre/Departements/Dept-26/26-05.html); [Air Element](https://www.air-element.com/en/experiment-a-paragliding-tandem-flight-above-the-diois/)
- **Pontias (Nyons)**: a **cool morning wind that arrives at Nyons**, blowing in the morning and "refreshing the beginning of the day". Legend places its origin at a rock near the chapelle Notre-Dame de Réparat. — [Vivre à Nyons – Nyons en 1 jour](https://www.vivreanyons.fr/que-faire-nyons/nyons-1-jour/)
- **End-of-day reversal (Gapençais)**: an Alpes-Envol site sheet notes that **when the breeze drops at the end of the day and the synoptic wind takes over, the effective orientation can change**. The site of Rabou is oriented **W / NW**. — [Alpes-Envol – fiche site de Rabou (PDF)](https://www.alpes-envol.fr/medias/files/site.de.rabou.pdf.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Durance breeze runs up the Durance (S→N from Sisteron to Tallard, then SW→NE through the Espinasses/dam corridor and over the lake toward Savines and Embrun). From the timing statements it peaks about 13h–17h in summer. The 30–40 km/h figure is documented for the Sisteron cluse. At Embrun/Serre-Ponçon landings, "forte" probably means 25–35 km/h with turbulence after 14–15h. That is my interpretation, not a sourced number.
- Saint-Vincent-les-Forts flies in NW/Mistral because its W/NW-facing takeoff sits in the low Durance–Ubaye corridor, sheltered by the higher reliefs, while Dévoluy and Chabre are exposed. Pilots in the region use it as the "Mistral fallback".
- Aspres (S-facing) is a breeze/south-wind site. Its 14h–16h launch window closes for the same reason the Durance landings get rough: the afternoon breeze maximum.

### Gaps
- No sourced numeric speeds for the Durance breeze at Tallard, Embrun or Saint-Vincent, the Buëch breeze at Laragne/Serres, the Drôme breeze at Die, the Ubaye breeze at Barcelonnette, or the Guil in Queyras.
- No source found for the **Serre-Ponçon lake breeze** (timing, direction, interaction with the Durance breeze) or for **Col Bayard** venturi effects. The latter is a known N–S pass between Gap and Champsaur, but nothing could be retrieved.
- No source on **Foehn / Lombarde / E-wind** effects in Ubaye or Queyras (Italian-border sectors where E/Lombarde is critical). Not retrievable this session.

## Q3. What are the soaring and thermal "classics" and record-flight references in this region?

### Takeoff and soaring classics

#### Takeaway
**Montagne de Chabre (Laragne)** is the regional reference. It is an FFVL international site with N and S takeoffs, soarable in light N wind and in the southern breeze. It was selected in **2017 for the Championnat de France de distance**, and it hosts triangles of about **193 km with ceilings of 3 300 m**. **Saint-Vincent-les-Forts** is the W/NW and late-afternoon classic. **Céüse cliffs** are the thermal motor used by Gap-Tallard gliders. **Aspres–Longeagne** is the south-wind/breeze site. **Couspeau**, **Glandasse**, **Mont Guillaume** and **Charance** are the regional hike-and-fly / cross launches.

  - **LES FEIGNANTS [13534]**: 44.2987 N, 5.7668 E, 1 283 m; 426 takeoffs in 2023. — [FFVL 13534](https://federation.ffvl.fr/terrain/13534)
  - **TABLE D'ORIENTATION [13535]**: 44.3031 N, 5.7761 E, 1 262 m. **"3 décollages nord"**: 1 suited to paragliders, 2 rather to hang gliders. — [FFVL 13535](https://federation.ffvl.fr/terrain/13535)
  - **TABLE D'ORIENTATION 2 [13746]**: 44.30438 N, 5.77936 E, 1 219 m. — [FFVL 13746](https://federation.ffvl.fr/sites_pratique/voir/13746)

#### Inferences
- Record-type flights in this region start from Chabre (early start, wide Buëch landing options, ceilings ~3 300 m). They loop through the Buëch / Dévoluy / Céüse / Durance reliefs. The glider circuit Céüse – Pic de Bure – Dévoluy – Charance traces the high, dependable thermal line north of Gap that paragliders also use.
- Saint-Vincent is the late-afternoon and NW-day complement. Mont Guillaume (Embrun) is the high-ceiling Durance launch, to be used from midday before the afternoon breeze makes the lakeside landing rough.

#### Gaps
- No documented waypoints or distances for the classic routes named in the brief (Laragne–Dévoluy–Champsaur, Céüse–Embrun–Briançon, Saint-Vincent–Ubaye, Diois–Vercors sud). XContest, Cross Country Magazine and CFD pages were blocked or out of search budget.
- No confirmed list of regional records (e.g. 200–300 km FAI triangles from Laragne/Saint-André-d'Embrun, or Aspres hang-gliding records). The [action-parapente.fr record article](https://action-parapente.fr/les-plus-gros-records-de-france-de-distance-en-parapente-en-ligne-droite/) surfaced, but no content was returned.
- No coordinates retrieved for Céüse paraglider takeoffs, Superdévoluy, Saint-André-d'Embrun, Chorges/Mont Morgon, Savines, Laye/Col Bayard, Chaillol, Serres/Veynes, Die (Croix de Justin), Col du Rousset, Couspeau, Col d'Ey, Pra-Loup/Le Sauze, or any Queyras site.

## Q4. Sector-by-sector status (what the JSON contains)

### Takeaway
The JSON (`data/hautes_alpes_sud.json`) has **9 massif entries**. The best documented are Buëch–Laragne–Chabre, Serre-Ponçon–Embrunais, Baronnies and Diois. Queyras and Ubaye are thin. Breezes along the Ubaye, Guil and Drôme are included only as **low-confidence "déduction" items** (physically standard up-valley flow) so the map has a placeholder.
